How a smart aircraft works

Sense. Think. Act.

Every smart aircraft runs the same loop, over and over.

  1. Sense

    Sensors (gyro, accelerometer, GPS, barometer, camera) measure what the plane is doing.

  2. Think

    A flight controller decides how to move the controls.

  3. Act

    The same servos and ESC that students fit carry out each decision.

Key point

Autopilot is not the same as AI

Autopilot

Follows rules we program.

vs
AI

In the air

How AI makes RC planes better

Self-levelling

The plane returns to level flight when the pilot lets go of the sticks.

Auto-tuning

The flight controller adjusts its own settings for smoother control.

Return to home

With GPS, the aircraft can fly itself back if the radio signal is lost.

Obstacle detection

A camera and AI model spot obstacles in the flight path.

Predicting failures

Patterns in flight logs warn of a weak battery or failing motor early.

Smarter design

Simulation tests many wing and tail shapes before anything is built.

On the ground

Where AI and drones help people

Agriculture

Spotting crop stress and spraying only where it is needed.

Search & rescue

Searching large areas quickly after floods and disasters.

Medical delivery

Carrying vaccines and medicines to hard-to-reach health centres.

Infrastructure inspection

Checking bridges, power lines and towers without risky climbs.

Environment & wildlife

Counting animals and mapping forests and coastlines.

Airline safety

Analysing flight data to find risks before they cause problems.

Close to home

Medicine from the Sky, Telangana

Telangana's "Medicine from the Sky" project began in Vikarabad in September 2021. The first drone carried vaccines to a health centre.

After the workshop

A student project path

The workshop is the start. Students can take the next steps in your Atal Tinkering Lab or computer lab.

  1. Fly the basic plane

    Start with the trainer built in the workshop.

  2. Add a flight controller

    Fit a controller running open-source ArduPilot or INAV, with GPS.

  3. Log flight data

    Record what the sensors measure on every flight.

  4. Train a simple model

    Find patterns in the data and present the project at a science fair.
