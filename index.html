Why schools choose us

Real engineering, on your campus, with no hassle

Students learn by building something that actually flies. You provide the space. We bring everything else.

Learning by doing

Physics and maths come alive as students build a real aircraft.

AI, made real

Students see how AI flies smart drones, with a lab project to follow.

Safety first

Only our pilots fly, students stay behind a marked line, and airspace is checked.

No hassle

We bring every tool and part. The school provides a hall and a ground.

What you will get

Six modules, from first principles to first flight

Each module builds on the last, so by Day 2 every team has the knowledge to assemble and check their own aircraft.

01

Flight basics

Forces of flight, lift, airfoils and the types of RC planes.

02

Anatomy & control

Aircraft parts, control surfaces and stability.

03

Electronics & power

Motor, ESC, servos, LiPo battery and radio.

04

Materials & design

Build materials and sizing a trainer aircraft.

05

Fabrication

Build plans for the fuselage, wing and tail.

06

AI & aviation

How AI makes aircraft smarter and helps people.
