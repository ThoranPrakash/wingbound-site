Safety

Safety comes first, every time

Clear rules for flying, batteries, tools and supervision, so principals and parents can say yes with confidence.

  • Who fliesOnly our pilots
  • AltitudeBelow 400 ft
  • RangeLine of sight
  • Under 18Signed consent
On flight day

Pilots fly. Students watch from behind the line.

The flight demo happens on an open ground of at least 100 m × 50 m. Before we confirm a date, we visit to check the ground and look up your campus on the DGCA Digital Sky airspace map.

Students who want to try the controls do so on a trainer link. The instructor holds the master radio and can take over at any moment.

Our rules

Three layers of safety

Flying

  • Only experienced WingBound pilots fly. Students stay behind a marked flight line.
  • The aircraft (about 800 g) is flown within line of sight, below 400 ft.
  • We check your campus on the DGCA Digital Sky airspace map before confirming a date.
  • Students try the controls only through a trainer link the instructor can override.
  • No flying over people, buildings or roads.

Batteries & tools

  • LiPo batteries are charged only by instructors, in fire-safe bags.
  • Propellers are fitted only for the flight demo.
  • Safety glasses for cutting.
  • A supervised glue station.

People & paperwork

  • First-aid kit on site.
  • A school teacher present throughout.
  • Signed parental consent for every student under 18.
  • Student photos are shared only with parental consent.
Before the date

What we check before anyone flies

  1. Airspace check

    We look up your campus on the DGCA Digital Sky airspace map before confirming a date.

  2. Site visit

    We visit to check the hall and the ground.

  3. Consent forms

    The school shares consent forms with parents. Every student under 18 needs one signed.

  4. On the day

    First-aid kit on site, a teacher present, and propellers fitted only for the flight demo.

Questions about safety?
