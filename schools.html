For Schools & Colleges

Easy to host. Hard to forget.

We bring every tool and part. You provide a hall, a ground and a teacher. Here is how it works.

Who provides what

A clear split, agreed up front

We bring

  • All electronics
  • Airframe materials
  • Build plans
  • Tools
  • Safety glasses
  • Fire-safe battery bags
  • Slides and worksheets
  • Certificates

The school provides

  • A hall or lab with tables and power sockets
  • A projector
  • An open ground at least 100 m × 50 m
  • One teacher present throughout
  • Signed consent forms
How booking works

Four simple steps

  1. Choose

    Choose a package and dates.

  2. Site visit

    We visit to check the hall and ground.

  3. Paperwork

    We send an agreement and invoice. The school shares consent forms with parents.

  4. Workshop

    We run the workshop and share photos and certificates.
